# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or
experience.

**Expected:** be respectful, assume good intent, give specific and useful feedback, and
accept correction gracefully.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing someone's
private information, or sustained disruption.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, and may block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.
